# Anthropic

AI safety company and creator of Claude and the Model Context Protocol.

## Overview

Anthropic is an AI safety company focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. Founded by former OpenAI researchers, the company develops Claude (a family of large language models) and supporting infrastructure including the [[Model Context Protocol (MCP)]].

## Products & Protocols

### AI Models
- **Claude** family of LLMs (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku)
- Focus on helpfulness, harmlessness, and honesty (HHH framework)

### Protocols & Infrastructure
- [[Model Context Protocol (MCP)]] — Open standard for AI-data source integration (launched 2026)
- [[Claude Desktop]] — Reference implementation of MCP

## Notable Contributions

### Protocol Design
- Created MCP to standardize context provisioning for LLMs
- Open-sourced MCP specification and SDKs
- Fostered community of 50+ MCP server implementations

### Engineering Practices
- Published technical overviews of MCP architecture
- Emphasis on [[Capability-Based Security]] in protocol design
- Transparent about performance characteristics
